If the Democrats announced a platform that spelled out exactly how they intend to fix what Trump & Co have broken, they’d (a) stymie many of Trump’s efforts, and (b) put themselves on a sure path to win. But they don’t. Harris, the notional head of the party, pissed away the early part of this year drafting her memoir because that matters more than the demolition of the institutions that prop up the govt’s legitimacy in the eyes of the citizenry and the world; and she’s spent the time since it was published doing a lite promo tour and demonstrating for all to see that she refuses to rethink her loss. Schumer is utterly lost in the minutiae of suppressing the progressive wave that is the party’s only chance (hence *the country’s* only chance). Jeffries? Who can even tell with that guy? So, at present, the Dems don’t have any real leadership as a party, just a motley band of “centrists” obsessing over their own bailiwicks. So, even at this late stage, aside from Mamdani and whoever Trump is putting in the news by attacking, they have *no* plans for fix what Trump & Co have broken. Revising the tax code to make it more fair? Not with them in charge. Fixing DHS/CBP/ICE? Nope. Rebuilding HHS and its vital component agencies? Nada. Investigating DOGE? Ferreting out and punishing the corruption that’s run amok? Restoring a semblance of faith in the DOJ? Reinstituting DOE? Prosecuting the leaders responsible for war crimes? Not a word. 

Don’t imagine for one moment the centrists will have *anything* to say about any of this unless they’re forced to do so. And the only way to force that is to force them out of the office they’re squatting in.
