I don’t think many people grasp the extent to which Trump & Co are *depleting* the US in every way. The $40 TRILLION debt and running out of “smart munitions” are just the tips of the icebergs: there’s also the strategic petroleum reserve, which is scraping the bottom of the barrel so to speak (60% used up — so low that we’ll start hearing about the structural integrity of the salt mines where it’s stored), the helium reserve (which Biden frittered and T&Co should have *restored*), etc, etc. And then of course there’s the brazen destruction of institutional resources (thanks to DOGE along with willing executioners like Linda McMahon), the destruction of natural resources, of geopolitical resources (alliances, multilateral entities, goodwill, etc). When they’re finally pried out of the White House, they’ll strip the place clean. And so it will fall to the next administration to try to recover and rebuild all that. The “corporate” Dems and their allies should be seen as so many Yeltsins, ensconced in their wood-paneled studies and drunk on their own inertia. Even beginning to prop the country up again will require astonishing amounts of wealth, so we need to be asking where it’ll come from. There are two basic options: either (a) austerity to a degree we’ve never imagined in the US or (b) excessively wealthy people. That, ultimately, is what this DSA-versus-the-establishment fight is all about. (Image from Barry Lyndon to make you look and also because it’s more than a little apropos.) The first excessively wealthy people to go after should be T&Co themselves: seize or freeze their assets — *all* their assets — until they can prove they obtained them legitimately, just like the cops routinely do to drivers and more every single day.
