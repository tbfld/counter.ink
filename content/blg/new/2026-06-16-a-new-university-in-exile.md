Almost every word of Feyzi Ismail’s Al-Jazeera piece “[The slow death of Goldsmiths is a warning to British universities](https://www.aljazeera.com/opinions/2026/6/15/the-slow-death-of-goldsmiths-is-a-warning-to-british-universities)” (15 June 2026) applies to the New School and most of them to US higher ed as a whole. But unlike the rest of US HE, the New School spent decades wanking itself to sleep with fantasies about its heroic past as “the university in exile.” But who or what really makes up a university? Is it the real estate and architecture? Nope. The letterhead, signage, and other branding ‘assets’? Nope. The faceless administrators with job titles that could’ve been generated with one of those ping-pong lottery contraption? Nope. The faculty, students, and staff? YES. So now, while the current administration is busy butchering the New School while claiming to save it, would be really good time for New School faculty to declare a *new* university in exile. My $5 says Mamdani’s administration would find some good space and a bit of funding somewhere. “IMPOSSIBLE!” you say? On the contrary. I’m not at all naive about the myriad issues — budgetary, legal, curricular, procedural, regulatory — involved in running a university; if anything, I probably know more about them than you do. A New U wouldn’t need to have formal admissions, grant degrees, be accredited, or much else — not initially, at least. All it’d really need to do is a fucking great job of building an educational resource that meets our moment. In fact, I’ll put my money where my mouth is: if anyone’s game, my time is yours.
