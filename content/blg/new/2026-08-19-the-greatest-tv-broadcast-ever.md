 Bets to treat this meditation on [Luther Blissett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luther_Blissett_(pseudonym)) as self-explanatory

![[luther-blissett-fantasy-footballer-2004.mp4]]

I was promoted to post this today by this odd apparition (🎩 Tim Mitchell): an image, attributed to the not-really-existent “Andy Warhol & Co. Ltd.” (Warhol died in 1987) rendition of a composite image generated in 1994 by Andrea Alberti and Edi Bianco by morphing two pre-WW2 portraits, to create a synthetic avatar for the synthetic “open pseudonym” [Luther Blissett](https://web.archive.org/web/20080421172856/http://www.t-h-e-n-e-t.com/html/_film/pers/_pers_blisset.htm). *See also:* Marco Deseriis’s [_Improper Names: Collective Pseudonyms from the Luddites to Anonymous_](https://books.google.com/books?id=1ih0DwAAQBAJ) (Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press, 2015).
