Listening to the radio is probably the one basic activity that’s been most irretrievably, viscerally degraded by “technology.”

1990: turn on radio, listen, maybe twiddle tuning

2026: realize that because you dislike having to do this stuff deeply so you never actually totally figured out the best way to get your phone to talk to your music situation — after many years, a wifi-enabled amp that at least doesn’t require any subscription etc BS — so try two streaming services (need to get rid of one but dread the family debate) to see if they have it live, find four million random-ass playlist album song what-the-f—-ever media object thing listings (what even is going on any more FFS?), so open the “app store” (please kill me now) and scroll (quickly) through (OMG) the (again) major (JFC app after app after app) radio-related apps you trashed long ago because they all suck shit, realize 15 minutes has gone by and your initial impulse — listening to the all-day Joao Gilbert fest on the college radio station a few blocks away, of which you have many fond memories — has been “enshittified” a dozen ways from Sunday, which is made even worse by your bilious hatred of the word “enshittified.” Realize you aren’t even back square one, you’re like way farther back in wherever that expression came from,
